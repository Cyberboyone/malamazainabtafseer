## Full description
Listen to the complete Tafseer 1447 series taught by Malama Zainab Jaafar
Mahmud, fully offline. All 18 parts (about 17 hours of audio) are built
into the app, so you can listen anywhere — no internet connection required
after installation.

Features:
• 18 offline Tafseer lessons, ~57 minutes each
• Auto-plays the next part (playlist mode)
• Resume from where you stopped — every lesson saves its position
• Background playback with lock-screen and notification controls
• Shuffle, loop, and seek controls
• Simple, clean and battery-friendly design

The app is free and supported by ads.
